## One-line workshop progression

```text
Day 1: Ground answers in evidence
   ↓
Day 2: Add bounded actions, evaluation tooling, and MCP integration
   ↓
Day 3: Measure, improve, secure, ship, and explain the result
```

The shared goal is not merely a completed demo. Every pair should leave with a reproducible project they understand, evidence of its behavior, an honest portfolio story, and enough engineering judgment to build the next project themselves.
